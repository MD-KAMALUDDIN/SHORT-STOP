# ShortStop User Guide

Complete guide to using ShortStop effectively.

## 📱 Getting Started

### First-Time Setup

1. **Install ShortStop** from Google Play Store or APK
2. **Open the app** and tap "Get Started"
3. **Grant Accessibility Permission**:
   - Tap "Enable Accessibility"
   - Find "ShortStop" in the list
   - Toggle it ON
   - Confirm the warning dialog
4. **Grant Overlay Permission**:
   - Tap "Enable Overlay"
   - Toggle "Allow display over other apps" ON
5. **Select Apps to Block**:
   - Tap "Select Apps"
   - Choose distracting apps (TikTok, Instagram, etc.)
   - Tap "Done"

### Quick Start

After setup, ShortStop works automatically:
- Open a blocked app → Wait 7 seconds → See intervention overlay
- Overlay shows for 30 seconds with a motivational quote
- After 30 seconds, you can dismiss or continue

## 🎯 Core Features

### 1. App Blocking

**How it works:**
- When you open a blocked app, ShortStop waits 7 seconds
- If you're still on the app after 7 seconds, an overlay appears
- The overlay shows a motivational quote and countdown (30 seconds)
- After 30 seconds, you can dismiss the overlay

**Why 7 seconds?**
- Allows quick, intentional use (checking a message)
- Blocks mindless scrolling and binge sessions

**Cooldown Period:**
- After each intervention, there's a 3-minute cooldown
- During cooldown, you won't see another overlay
- Prevents overlay spam while allowing conscious use

### 2. Study Mode

**Starting Study Mode:**
1. Tap the "Study Mode" button on the main screen
2. Study mode lasts 25 minutes (Pomodoro technique)
3. ALL selected apps are completely blocked during this time

**During Study Mode:**
- Opening a blocked app shows a study mode overlay
- Overlay cannot be dismissed until study mode ends
- Notification shows remaining time
- Focus on your work without distractions!

**Ending Study Mode:**
- Automatically ends after 25 minutes
- Or tap "End Study Mode" in the notification
- Study session count increases by 1

### 3. Categories

Organize your apps into three categories:

**Social Media** 📱
- TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, Snapchat, etc.
- Apps focused on social networking and content feeds

**Entertainment** 🎮
- YouTube, Netflix, Games, Spotify, etc.
- Apps for media consumption and gaming

**Other** 📦
- Any other distracting apps
- Shopping, news, browsers, etc.

**Filtering:**
- Tap category chips to filter your app list
- "All" shows all blocked apps
- Select a category to see only those apps

### 4. Statistics Dashboard

Track your progress with four key metrics:

**Streak** 🔥
- Days in a row you've used ShortStop
- Resets if you don't use the app for a day
- Build long streaks for higher ranks!

**Time Saved** ⏱️
- Estimated minutes saved from interventions
- Each intervention = ~30 seconds saved
- Adds up quickly over time!

**Study Sessions** 📚
- Number of 25-minute study modes completed
- Shows your commitment to focused work

**Interventions** 🛑
- Total times you've been interrupted
- Higher number = more awareness of habits

### 5. Rank System

Your rank is based on your overall progress:

## ⚙️ Settings

### App Management

**Adding Apps:**
1. Tap "Select Apps" button
2. Browse or search for apps
3. Tap apps to select them
4. Tap "Done" to save

**Removing Apps:**
1. Tap "Select Apps" button
2. Tap selected apps to deselect
3. Tap "Done" to save

**Changing Categories:**
1. Long-press an app in your list
2. Select new category from menu
3. App moves to new category

### Permissions

**Accessibility Service:**
- Required for app detection
- Can be disabled in Android Settings → Accessibility
- Disabling stops all blocking functionality

**Overlay Permission:**
- Required for intervention screens
- Can be disabled in Android Settings → Apps → ShortStop
- Disabling prevents overlays from showing

**Notifications:**
- Optional, only for study mode
- Shows remaining study time
- Can be disabled in Android notification settings

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Overlays Not Appearing

**Check Accessibility Service:**
1. Go to Android Settings → Accessibility
2. Find "ShortStop" in the list
3. Make sure it's toggled ON
4. If OFF, toggle it ON and confirm

**Check Overlay Permission:**
1. Go to Android Settings → Apps → ShortStop
2. Tap "Display over other apps"
3. Make sure it's allowed

**Restart the App:**
1. Force stop ShortStop in Android Settings
2. Open ShortStop again
3. Check if service is enabled in app

### App Not Blocking

**Verify App is Selected:**
- Open ShortStop
- Check if the app appears in your blocked list
- If not, add it via "Select Apps"

**Check Cooldown Period:**
- If you just saw an overlay, wait 3 minutes
- Cooldown prevents overlay spam

**Battery Optimization:**
- Some devices kill background services
- Go to Settings → Battery → ShortStop
- Disable battery optimization for ShortStop

### Study Mode Not Working

**Check Notification Permission:**
- Android 13+ requires notification permission
- Grant permission when prompted
- Or enable in Settings → Apps → ShortStop → Notifications

**Service Running:**
- Make sure accessibility service is enabled
- Check main screen shows "Service Enabled"

### Streak Not Updating

**Use App Daily:**
- Streak only increases if you use ShortStop each day
- Opening the app counts as daily use
- Missing a day resets streak to 1

**Check Date/Time:**
- Make sure device date/time is correct
- Incorrect time can affect streak tracking

### Statistics Not Accurate

**Database Sync:**
- Statistics update in real-time
- If not updating, restart the app
- Data is stored locally and persists

**Manual Reset:**
- Currently no way to reset statistics
- Reinstalling app will clear all data

## 🎓 Understanding the Science

### Why 7 Seconds?

Research shows the first few seconds of app use are often intentional (checking notifications, replying to messages). After 7 seconds, usage typically becomes mindless scrolling.

### Why 30 Seconds?

The 30-second pause creates a "pattern interrupt" - breaking the automatic behavior loop. This gives your brain time to consciously decide if you want to continue.

### Why 25 Minutes (Study Mode)?

Based on the Pomodoro Technique, 25 minutes is optimal for focused work. It's long enough for deep work but short enough to maintain concentration.

### The Psychology

ShortStop uses:
- **Friction**: Adding a small barrier to automatic behavior
- **Awareness**: Making unconscious habits conscious
- **Positive Reinforcement**: Motivational quotes and progress tracking
- **Gamification**: Ranks and streaks for motivation
